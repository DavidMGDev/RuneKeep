# RuneKeep — Product

register: product

## Users
Daggerheart TTRPG players using the app **at the table**, mid-session, often one-handed on a phone.
They glance at it constantly to read and tap resource trackers (HP, Stress, Hope, Armor) while playing.

## Product purpose
A living digital character sheet. The job-to-be-done is **fast, reliable tracking of resources during
play** — tap a heart off, spend Hope, mark Stress — plus quick reference to abilities/inventory (cards).

## Brand & tone
Heroic dark-fantasy, premium and confident. Bold, sharp, a little ceremonial. Not cute, not corporate.
The aesthetic is set by the AELIANA mockup: **flat, chamfered, red + gold on near-black/ivory**.

## Strategic principles
1. **Icons first.** The resource ICONS (Hearts, Armor, Stress, Hope) are the most important pixels on
   the screen — large, always visible, thumb-tappable. Their numbers matter less than the icons.
2. **Then traits, then character info.** Trait modifiers are prominent; identity (name/class/portrait)
   is supporting, capped at ~30% of the screen.
3. **One screen, no scroll.** Everything a player tracks fits on one screen; the card deck lives at the
   bottom edge and expands over the sheet on demand.
4. **Frames give structure.** Every block sits inside a chamfered frame; nothing floats "lost."

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards; rounded-corner card grids; pastel/neutral palettes.
- Tiny, dense, unreadable stat rows (the failure mode we are fixing).
