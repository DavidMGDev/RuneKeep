RuneKeep v0.34.8 - Cards you can print, cards made of one picture, copies that are really one card, and Back that asks first.

PRINTING

- SHARE AS PDF. Pick any cards, from any decks at once, and lay them out nine to a US Letter sheet, three by three, at true playing-card size (2.5 by 3.5 inches). Cut them out and they fit a sleeve. A phone writes the file and hands it to the share sheet; a browser opens its own print dialogue, where Save as PDF is waiting.
- THE CARDS GO ON THE PAGE AT FULL RESOLUTION. A forged card is 750 by 1050 pixels and a printed card at 300 DPI is 750 by 1050 pixels, so nothing is stretched to fit.

SHARING

- THE CARDS PANEL SHARES. Its "Duplicate" button is "Share" now, which is the one place you can pick cards out of every category at once. Copying moved to where copying already lives: the Move sheet's "copy instead of move" checkbox, here as everywhere else.
- FILES CARRY THEIR PICTURES. A card or a stack shared as a file used to arrive with its art blank, because the file held a path into the sending phone rather than the image itself. So did every expansion ever shared. Both pack the artwork in now.

CARDS MADE OF ONE PICTURE

- A CARD CAN NOW BE A WHOLE IMAGE, for faces you have already finished somewhere else (the official Daggerheart card creator exports one). "Use a whole card image" in the advanced editor. It still has a name, a type and modifiers, because that is how the app files it and applies it; it simply prints as your picture, edge to edge, the way the published cards do.
- A PACK FROM A FOLDER. "Add cards from images" in an expansion turns every picture you pick into its own card in one go. They arrive unnamed on purpose: naming them and saying what each one is comes next.
- YOU CANNOT SHARE A HALF-FINISHED PACK. Saving one is fine. Sending one with an unnamed card, or a domain card with no domain, is not: on the other person's phone that is a card nobody can use.

COPIES

- A COPY IS THE SAME CARD, NOT A SECOND ONE. Equip either and both are equipped, edit either and both change, and the modifier counts once. The Modifiers panel now says which one you are looking at, and how many places the card is in.
- DELETING THE ORIGINAL NO LONGER TAKES ITS COPIES WITH IT. The card survives and moves into a copy's place. Only when the last of them is deleted does the card go, and then it is in the Trash where it belongs.
- TWO OF THE SAME CARD ARE TWO CARDS. Two potions picked up separately were being welded together: enabling one enabled the other, and only one of them counted. They are separate now, unless you deliberately copied one.

CARD EDITING

- BACK ASKS FIRST. Pressing Back with something written on a card offers to save it, leave it, or keep writing, instead of throwing it away without a word. In the quick flow, a single character is enough to be asked.
- THE QUICK FLOW'S DESCRIPTION BOX GROWS with what you type, the way the full editor's does.

DECKS

- YOUR CLASS DECK SITS LAST. Beastform, Companion and Martial Form come after Notes now, so a Druid steps Arsenal, Inventory, Notes, Beastform rather than through everything else to reach the deck the class gave them.
- ARCHIVE IS THE VAULT.

FIXES

- RESTORING FROM THE TRASH WORKS. The prompt asking which deck a card goes back into was only ever drawn while a card move happened to be in progress, so pressing Restore did nothing at all: no dialogue, no card, no message, and the entry stayed in the list.
