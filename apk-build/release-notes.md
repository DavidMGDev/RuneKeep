RuneKeep v0.34.0 - The moodboard: double tap your portrait for a canvas that belongs to your character.

THE MOODBOARD

- DOUBLE TAP YOUR PORTRAIT to open a flat canvas that belongs to this character. Place images on it, drag them with one finger, use two to resize and turn at once, and double tap an image for its menu: delete, duplicate, bring to front, send to back, centre.
- IT OPENS LOCKED, every time. Hold the lock for a second, and watch it fill, before anything can move. Locking again is one tap. Leaving always locks it, so coming back is never a surprise.
- TURNING AN IMAGE SNAPS TO RIGHT ANGLES, and dragging one snaps to the middle of the canvas. Both grab early and let go late, so straightening is effortless and turning past a snap on purpose still works.
- THE LIST BUTTON SHOWS EVERY IMAGE with a thumbnail, so nothing can be lost. Centre brings anything back to the middle and to the front, and layers can be moved from there too.
- YOUR BOARD TRAVELS WITH THE CHARACTER. Export a character and its moodboard goes with it.
- The character sheet unloads while the board is open, so the canvas gets the whole device.

CARDS

- CARD DESCRIPTIONS FIT ON A PHONE. This is the third attempt and the first with a measurement behind it. If you have made your phone's text larger, Android was scaling every word on a card AFTER the app had worked out a size to fit the card, so the text overflowed by however much your setting adds. Cards now draw at the size they were laid out at. Everything else on your phone is unaffected.
- EACH HOPE AND FEAR ANCESTRY APPEARS ONCE. Gnome, Earthkin, Emberkin, Skykin, Tidekin and Aetheris were each listed twice in the creator, once from the base catalogue and once from the expansion. That is also what was making the end of the ancestry deck flicker: two copies of a card share one identity, and the app could not tell their pictures apart.

THE TRASH

- DELETING ANY CARD PUTS IT IN THE TRASH, not just the ones you wrote. A deleted armor never appeared there before.
- RESTORING ASKS WHICH DECK to put the card back in, the same way every other card in the app is filed.
- RESTORING ACTUALLY WORKS. Deleting a card you wrote both removed it and hid it, and Restore only undid the first half, so the card came back to a place that went on hiding it.

ELSEWHERE

- THE TIMELINE LOADS AS YOU SCROLL and no longer slows down at a hundred entries.
- CATEGORY ROWS PRESS LIKE BUTTONS, the current one is marked in gold rather than by a word, and the lit colour is a calmer bronze instead of the red the app uses for damage. A category that cannot be turned on now says why.
- THE HOMEBREW FILTER ONLY APPEARS WHERE THERE IS HOMEBREW, and clears itself when you move to a category that has none.
- THE PORTRAIT ANSWERS THE MOMENT YOU TOUCH IT, and holding it to replace the photo starts showing progress straight away.
