RuneKeep v0.34.6 - The pair reads right, the dark colours have names, images can be pinned, and the timeline can be cut down to size.

THE HOPE AND FEAR DICE

- THE NUMBERS SIT IN THE MIDDLE OF EACH DIE. They hung from the top of the token and were pushed down, so they read low and drifted with the font.
- THE PAIR GOES BACK TO ITS RESTING SIZE ON ITS OWN. The hold grows the whole token, and a pair's roll animates each die instead, so it was left large until you touched it again.
- STILL NO TAP TO STEP. A pair is rolled by holding it, and by nothing else, which is what they are for.

COLOUR

- THE DARK END HAS REAL NAMES. Two whole rows read "Black" and vivid dark colours read "Dark Slate Gray", because the standard colour list has almost nothing dark in it and every deep colour matched the same few names. Below a third lightness the colour is described instead: Deep Teal, Midnight Violet, Blackened Green.
- A DARK SATURATED COLOUR IS NEVER CALLED GREY. Only the washed-out ones are.

THE MOODBOARD

- THE IMAGE MENU STAYS OPEN. It could appear for a frame and vanish while its buttons stayed where they were, so the next tap deleted an image you were only trying to look at. The guard that was meant to prevent it was armed one paint too late.
- IMAGES CAN BE LOCKED, from the menu or from the images list. A locked image cannot be moved, resized, turned or double tapped, and it is unlocked from the images list.
- THE PORTRAIT'S EDGES ARE DRAWN, once the board is your portrait. A portrait keeps a tall slice of the middle, and there was no way to know where it fell until you had left. "Draw frame limits" in the images list turns the guides off.

THE TIMELINE

- IT SCROLLS IN A BROWSER. A swipe that began on a row was taken by that row's hold, so the list stayed put and the rewind bar filled instead.
- A COMPACT BUTTON, next to the title. It throws away everything except your character's creation and each level up, leaving the milestones worth rewinding to. It asks first, and it cannot be undone.

CARDS

- THE DESCRIPTION BOX GROWS with what you write, rather than holding a wall of text in a small window you have to scroll.
