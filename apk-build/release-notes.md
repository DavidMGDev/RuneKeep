RuneKeep v0.43.4 - A homebrew class keeps its cards.

THE BIG ONE

- A SHAMAN STAYS A SHAMAN AFTER IT IS SAVED. Every time a character was opened, the app threw away the parts of its homebrew cards it did not recognise, and that included what makes a class a class. The next save made it permanent. That is why an existing Shaman went back to reading "Druid" and found its class pages in the Inventory. Nothing is thrown away now.
- EXISTING CHARACTERS ARE REPAIRED WHEN THEIR SHEET OPENS, from the pack on your device. Keep the pack installed and an affected Shaman comes back whole the first time you open it.
- A CLASS'S FEATURE CARDS ARE HANDED OUT. Vodou Healing Powers, and every card written as a feature of a homebrew class, now sits in the Arsenal beside the class's pages, with its counter working. A feature tied to one subclass only goes to characters of that subclass. A feature set to land in its own category arrives in that category.
- FEATURE CARDS WEAR THEIR CLASS'S BANNER on the sheet, as they do in the card editor.

SHARING

- AN IMPORTED PACK KEEPS EVERYTHING ITS AUTHOR WROTE. The same clean-up ran on import, so a homebrew class shared with a friend arrived with no class details, no counters and no campaign settings. All three arrive now.

ADD GEAR

- A CLASS PAGE FROM ADD GEAR IS THE CLASS CARD, with the class's title and banner, not the bare page its author edits. It goes to the Arsenal, as a printed class card does.

CARD EDITOR

- "BELONGS TO A CLASS" NAMES EACH CLASS ONCE. A class with two pages was offered as Shaman, Shaman, Shaman.
