RuneKeep v0.32.0 - domain cards have their real names, seven of them finally do something, you can mute a card without unequipping it, dice roll when you hold them, and the app tells you when there is a newer one.

CARDS

- DOMAIN CARDS ARE CALLED WHAT THEY ARE CALLED. Blade 8 is Frenzy, Bone 2 is Ferocity, Grace 9 is Master of the Craft. All 231 of them, base game and Void, read off the card art itself (the rulebook stores these cards as pictures, so there was no text to pull from). The names show in the carousel, the gallery, the catalogue and every modifier line.
- SEVEN CARDS THAT WERE DOING NOTHING NOW DO SOMETHING. Bone-Touched, Frenzy, Deadly Focus, Shadowhunter, Full Surge, Eldritch Flesh and Overwhelming Aura all had a number in their rules text and no entry in the modifier table, so equipping them took a loadout slot and changed nothing.
- TOGGLE, ON EQUIPPED DOMAIN CARDS. Next to Modifiers on a focused card. The card stays equipped and keeps counting toward your five, but its bonuses stop applying. Cards like Frenzy are only true for one scene and were holding you to a choice between a wrong number and a wrong loadout. The corner check turns grey while a card is muted, so you can see it at a glance. Anything permanent keeps applying, because muting cannot sensibly be stronger than putting the card away.
- THE "#" BUTTON, ON CARDS THAT ASK FOR A NUMBER. Ferocity gives Evasion equal to the Hit Points your target marked, which is not on your sheet and never will be, so the card asks. Tap #, type it, done. The number belongs to that card alone. Card authors get it too: "Number Input" is now a modifier variable alongside Level, Tier, Proficiency and Spellcast, and it multiplies and divides like any of them.
- "CURRENT STRESS" IS A MODIFIER VARIABLE. Eldritch Flesh gives +1 Armor Score for every 2 Stress you have marked, and it updates as you mark and clear it.
- "OVERWRITE", A NEW CHECKBOX BESIDE PERMANENT. The stat becomes the value, discarding everything else contributing to it. Overwhelming Aura needed it: your Presence is equal to your Spellcast trait, which is not a bonus, it is a replacement.
- MASTER OF THE CRAFT SAYS WHAT TO DO. Equipping it now tells you to open Modifiers and choose your bonus, instead of appearing to do nothing.

VITALITY, AND THE REST OF THE PICK-TWO CARDS

- THE PICKER HAS A PANEL NOW. It was bare text floating over a lightly dimmed character sheet: unreadable, and tapping any line of it closed the dialog instead of choosing. A tap on a label now chooses the label.
- A PERMANENT CARD'S CORNER IS BRONZE, NOT RED. Red meant equipped, which is the one thing Vitality is not: its own text tells you to put it in your vault and keep the benefit.

TOKENS AND DICE

- HOLD A DIE TO ROLL IT. It swells, the number cross-fades to a new one, and it settles. Holding a die used to delete it, which was the wrong verb for the object.
- KEEP HOLDING AND SWIPE TO THROW IT. The die flies off in the direction you swiped and is gone. Deliberately not twitchy, and while a die is rolling under your finger the card can no longer be closed by accident.
- THE CHECKMARK ON EQUIPPED CARDS IS WHOLE. Its two strokes stopped on the vertex instead of crossing it, and the two rounded ends left a notch exactly where the check should come to a point.
- THE NUMBER ON A d4 SITS 2px FURTHER RIGHT.

TEXT AND KEYPADS

- A DESCRIPTION FULL OF BLANK LINES NO LONGER RUNS INTO THE FOOTER. Past a certain length the card gave up, held the type at its floor and reported a line count that was a fiction; Android does not honour a line height below the font's own, so those lines rendered taller there than the arithmetic said. It tightens the leading before it shrinks the type now, and it fits by construction, with every line kept.
- PLUS AND MINUS ON BOTH KEYPADS. Either side of the number, on the rest keypad and the damage keypad. Adjusting a figure by one is far more common than typing it again. Small glyphs, large touch targets.

SHARING AND UPDATES

- "SHARE FILE" WHERE IT REALLY SHARES. On a phone the file goes straight to any app on the device, in the browser as well as the app. On a desktop, where that is not possible, the button still says Export to File and still saves it, because promising a share that cannot happen is worse than a plain download.
- THE APP TELLS YOU WHEN THERE IS A NEWER ONE. A quiet strip on the main menu, and nothing at all when you are current. On Android it offers the download page; in a browser it clears the old files and reloads. It never downloads anything on its own.
