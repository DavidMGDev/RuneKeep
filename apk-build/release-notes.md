RuneKeep v0.50.7 - A heavier hand, livelier dice.

On the website (runekeep.pages.dev), on a computer:

- Re-arranging the first or last card of an endless deck slides it smoothly into place. It used to jump around the hand.
- A crit on your hand of cards now takes a near-full throw. The hit freezes as long as any other big hit, and the hand is knocked back instead of off the table; a few crits in a row still knock it off.
- Clicking a heart, Stress, Hope or Armor grows it from its normal size before it bursts, instead of jumping straight to full size.
- Dice you drop gently hop higher and spin more. Hard throws feel about the same as before, a little stronger.
- A Hope and Fear pair selected on its own catches fire when you hold it still, like any other die.

On your phone: nothing new in this version.

No character migration is required.

Sideload: enable Install unknown apps, then open the APK.
