RuneKeep v0.34.3 - A real colour picker, a back button that closes the moodboard, and a portrait you can put back.

COLOUR

- THERE IS A COLOUR PICKER NOW, for the moodboard's background and for a card's art. Forty odd swatches, laid out and tapped, with a ring on the one you are using. "Random" was never really random on the board: it walked a fixed set of seven, which is not a choice.
- THE SAME PICKER IS IN ADVANCED CARD CREATION AND CARD EDITING, with Surprise me inside it for when you do want the dice.
- THE BACKGROUND BUTTON READS AS SWATCHES. The palette drawing it had was unreadable at the size it is actually drawn.
- SETTING A COLOUR OVER A PICTURE ASKS FIRST, everywhere a colour can be set. One tap could throw away a photo you had just chosen.

THE MOODBOARD

- BACK CLOSES THE BOARD and returns you to your character sheet, saving on the way. It used to take you all the way out to the character list.
- THE LOCKED NOTICE NO LONGER COVERS THE LOCK. It said to press a button it was sitting on top of. It has its own line at the foot of the board.
- TURNING "USE AS PORTRAIT" OFF PUTS YOUR OLD PORTRAIT BACK, crop and all. One is kept, and the rewind screen now says plainly that anything older than that is not saved on file and will not come back.

CARDS

- THE CARD ART IS A CONTROL IN THE FULL EDITOR TOO. Tap it for a new colour, hold it for a picture, exactly like quick card creation. The labelled buttons are still there.
- A SHARED CARD KEEPS ITS TYPE. Your Story cards arrived on the other phone reading "Card", because the type was the one thing the transfer dropped.
- CHANGING A CARD'S TYPE REPAINTS IT. Editing only the type left the old card on the carousel, and a custom item ignored its type entirely.

ELSEWHERE

- HOLD THE "?" ON THE MAIN MENU to see which version you are running.
