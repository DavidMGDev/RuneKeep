RuneKeep v0.36.1 - Characterize gets a checklist, portraits get their corners back, and an adversary's difficulty becomes its Evasion.

CHARACTERIZE

- NO MORE EXPANSION QUESTION. Turning an adversary into a character no longer asks which packs it can draw from. Whatever you have enabled in the app's own expansion list is what it gets, which is the same place the Transform step already looked.
- SKIP MENU. Once a class is settled, a red button next to Random opens every step of creation as one checklist, on one screen, no scrolling. Check what this character does not need and press once. Steps you have already filled in are marked, and skipping one warns you first, because skipping puts a step back to its default: whatever the stat block carried, or nothing at all. When there is nothing left to answer the button says Skip and Forge instead.
- THE CLASS CAN BE SKIPPED, WHEN IT IS NOT DOING ANYTHING. A class exists on a sheet to supply hit points and Evasion. An adversary carrying both is already supplying them, so it is no longer forced to pick one. Leave either of those cards behind and the class comes straight back, un-skipping itself if it had been skipped.
- DIFFICULTY IS EVASION. An adversary's difficulty carries across as the number an attack has to beat, and it is held exactly: pick Bard, whose Evasion is 10, and a difficulty 14 ally still reads 14, because the difference is carried as its own modifier. Hit points and stress work the same way, as they already did.
- LEVEL SITS WITH INHERIT. Both are numbers the stat block worked out rather than choices you make, so they no longer sit either side of the first real decision.
- LEAVE THIS BEHIND. Cards you are not carrying were faded, and fading them let the cards behind show through where the carousel overlaps, which read as bright seams rather than as a dim card. They are drawn solid and greyed now, with a band saying so.
- ONE STAT PER LINE. A claw attack printed "Range: Very Close Damage: 1d12+2 phy physical Attack: +3" as one run. Each stat is its own row now, and the damage type is not said twice.
- A NAME THE APP MADE UP IS NOT A NAME. "Adversary #3" is left behind and the name field starts empty; a real name is carried across and the step counts as answered.
- SKIPPING RESPECTS THE STEP. A skipped step takes its default rather than nothing: traits arrive as whatever was inherited, or as zeroes when there was nothing to inherit.
- THE THREE NEW TABS FIT. Inherit, Level and Transform had no icon, so their tabs collapsed while every other one held one. They have their own now, and shorter names.
- A DM'S DRAFT STAYS A DM'S. A half-finished characterization is only ever offered back for the same adversary, so it can no longer turn up in front of a player starting an ordinary hero on the same device.

EVERY CAROUSEL

- OPENS ON THE LEFT OF THE MIDDLE. A step with two cards opened on the SECOND one, so the deck arrived already scrolled to the end and the unchosen card was behind you. Two cards now open on the left one, and an odd number still opens in the middle.

THE ENCOUNTER

- A DOWNWARD ARROW DOWNS, AN X REMOVES. Both were an X, which said "delete" for a control that does not delete: downing an adversary keeps it in the fight as Fallen. The X stays on a fallen row, where it really does remove.
- DIFFICULTY AT A GLANCE. It sits beside the damage thresholds with its own mark, without expanding the stat block.

SHARED FILES

- A .rune FROM ANOTHER APP OPENS AGAIN. Receiving a character over Quick Share, or tapping one in a file manager, either did nothing at all or said the file could not be read. Two separate faults. The read went through a file API that, for a shared file, looks the file up the wrong way and throws before it ever opens it, so the one operation that would have worked was never reached; shared files are now copied across through the system's own reader first. And a file handed over by an app you picked RuneKeep to open with is no longer put through the check that exists to ignore photos your own image picker hands back, which is what made a Quick Share open the app and sit on the menu.
- IT SAYS WHAT WENT WRONG. If a file still cannot be read, the message now names the reason instead of giving the same sentence for every possible cause.

THE GOLDEN GEAR

- 20% MORE SENSITIVE, ON PHONE AND IN A BROWSER. Standing on the last card of a deck and switching to the category on the left is the longest gesture there is: the whole deck back to the first card, then past the end far enough to arm the switch. It did not fit in one swipe. Now it does.

PORTRAITS

- THEY FOLLOW THEIR FRAMES. Every list in the app framed a portrait in a chamfered box and then drew a square picture over it. Nothing looked wrong until something moved, and the party sheet's roster dims on press, which uncovered four corners of frame that had been hiding under the picture all along. Portraits are cut to the same shape as their frames now, everywhere: the roster, the party sheet, the party editor, the encounter and the adversary library.
